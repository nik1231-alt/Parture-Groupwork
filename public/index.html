The shop behind this app

Parture designs, prints and sells 3D-printed spare parts, holders and accessories. The parts are printed in-house on the shop's own printer farm and sold on eBay, Amazon and the shop's own web shop.

What the shop already runs by software

Built by the shop itself over the last year, outside this course. It is the system this app plugs into.

Printer farm

A scheduler decides which printer prints which plate next, from open orders and stock. It watches every printer and keeps stock per part and colour.

Slicing

3D models are turned into print plates by a slicing worker, so a new part can go from model to printer without manual plate building.

Orders and shipping

Orders from all channels arrive in one place. Shipping labels for letters and parcels are created there, and the tracking number goes back to the marketplace.

Customer service

One inbox for every channel. A daily run reads each open case in full, decides and answers; refunds and replacements stay within fixed limits.

Catalogue and channels

One product master feeds eBay, Amazon and the web shop. Title, price and stock changes are passed on to the channels.

Controlling

Cash flow and contribution per product are computed from bank and marketplace data, so decisions rest on numbers and not on feeling.

New products

A daily run looks for demand on eBay, finds printable models with a free licence, checks them and prepares the listing.

Operations

Every automatic run reports to one board. A run that fails or stays silent shows up there instead of going unnoticed.

Where this project fits in

The gap: the shop could produce, ship and answer, but nobody looked systematically at the many eBay listings that buyers hardly ever see.

The Listing Lab (this app, the team project) closes it: it reads an hourly, whitelisted export of the catalogue, finds those listings, draws a test and a control group by lot, and moves the test listings through three levers: new title, ads, lower price. Nothing changes on eBay until an admin approves it.
