Get more impressions for your eBay listings

The focus is visibility: listings that buyers rarely see, whether they sell or not. Each one gets a ready-made next step for the title. You decide, publish it on eBay and log it here. Click rates and sales come later.

Change log

Every change you published is stored here with the old value, the new value, the date and the numbers at the start. After 30 days the app compares the listing with all unchanged listings and tells you whether the change helped. Only one change per listing at a time.
